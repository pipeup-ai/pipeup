Field notes · October

Autumn at the footbridge

What we found at the footbridge, and what to do before winter.

The river

The river rose twice that week, and both times the footbridge held. The second rise came within a hand of the deck boards, which is closer than any year since the bridge was rebuilt.

We recommend raising the deck by one course before the spring melt. The cost is modest and the work can be done in two days with the water low.

The path

The path on the north bank is washed out for about twenty metres. Walkers are cutting across the meadow, which is doing more harm than the river did.
