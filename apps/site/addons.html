Add-ons

Pipeup on its own needs none of these. It sends nothing anywhere, and most pages should stay that way.

An add-on is one extra script that the page's author chooses to add. Reviewers are never moved into one, and each asks before it does anything. The add-ons are in beta: the core alone is the stable choice.

Share

Comments reach everyone who has the page, not only the browser they were written in.

Sends
Comments, sealed in the reviewer's browser with a key that is only in the page's link, to a service the author picks: a PrivateBin or their own small mailbox server. The service never sees the words.
Asked first
Comments written before sharing stay on the device until the reviewer chooses to share them.
You choose
Where it goes. Pipeup never picks a service for you.
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@pipeup/share@next/dist/share.min.js"></script>
<html data-pipeup-share="https://…#…">

Voice

Dictate a comment instead of typing it.

Sends
Nothing of its own. Speech is turned into words by the browser's own engine, which in some browsers sends the audio to its maker.
Asked first
A short note before the first use, then the browser's own microphone prompt.
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@pipeup/voice@next/dist/voice.min.js"></script>

Live

See comments arrive as people write them, who else is on the page, and where they are looking.

Sends
Presence and comments straight between the reviewers' browsers. Public relays are used only to introduce them to each other, and see addresses, not words.
Asked first
Each reviewer is asked before connecting. Nothing is opened until they agree.
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@pipeup/live@next/dist/live.min.js"></script>
