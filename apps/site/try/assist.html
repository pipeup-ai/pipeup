Q3 launch plan

Try Assist

  1. Press Shift+Option+C (Shift+Alt+C on Windows) and comment on a paragraph, for example “Are we sure about the July date?” or “What about churn?”.
  2. Open the comment control's menu and turn on Assistant replies. It asks first, then reads the page.
  3. When something else on the page bears on your comment, it replies with a short line and a pill. Hover the pill to see the sentence; press it to go there.

Sends Nothing. The model runs on your device, and your browser may download it once.

Needs A computer with Chrome, which has a built-in model (about 22 GB of free disk once, shared with other sites).
