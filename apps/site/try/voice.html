Try Voice

  1. Press Shift+Option+C (Shift+Alt+C on Windows) to start commenting, then click a paragraph.
  2. In the comment box, press the microphone and say your comment. Your words appear as you speak; you check them, then send.

Sends Nothing of its own. Your browser's speech engine does the listening, and in some browsers it sends the audio to its maker. Voice asks first.

Needs A browser with speech recognition (Chrome, Edge or Safari). Without one, Voice stays off and says why.
