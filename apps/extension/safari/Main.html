Pipeup for Safari

Comment on any web page, then pass the comments to the person or AI agent who asked for your feedback.

Turn Pipeup on in Safari Settings, under Extensions.

Pipeup is on in Safari.

Pipeup is off. Turn it on in Safari Settings, under Extensions.

  1. In Safari, open a web page and press Pipeup in the toolbar.
  2. Choose Comment on this page, click what you want to comment on, type, and press Return.
  3. Press Pipeup again for your comments: Copy comments or Save comments to send them on.
