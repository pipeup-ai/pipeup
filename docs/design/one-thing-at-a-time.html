Visual spec · agreed and built 9 November 2026

One thing at a time in comment mode

In comment mode, while a thread is open or a comment has words in it, the page stops offering blocks: no outlines on hover, and a click on the page closes or returns rather than starting something new. The page offers blocks again as soon as that thread or comment is done. This is Pipeup's own behaviour, on every page, not only with the extension.

Esc, Cancel, Comment and closing the thread all end it, and blocks are offered again at once. The keyboard's block cursor follows the same rule: while a thread is open or a comment has words, Tab stays in it. Clicking a comment's highlight still opens that comment, in every case without words being written.

3. The spec change

In §5, beside the rule for selected words:

While a thread is open to read, no block is outlined or named on hover, and a click on the page closes it and does nothing else (a reply being written is kept); the next click chooses a block. A thread just written doesn't hold up the next comment. While a comment has words, blocks aren't offered and a click goes back to it. An empty comment box still moves to the block clicked.

4. Decisions (agreed 9 November 2026)

An empty comment box: move it, or close it first? Agreed: move it, as now. A box opened on the wrong block is the commonest slip, and one click fixes it. The other choice is one click to close the box and a second to choose again.

An open thread: close it, or close it and choose the block clicked? Agreed: only close it. Reading a thread and clicking away to dismiss it shouldn't start a comment. The other choice saves a click when the reviewer meant to comment next.
