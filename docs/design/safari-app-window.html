On Macs that say Preferences instead of Settings, the words follow, as today.

Rules it keeps

  • Light and dark, both complete, following the Mac. The drawing uses the same two palettes.
  • The window stays the same size and the state swaps ease in, nothing blinks.
  • Nothing new is collected or sent. The sample page is Pipeup's own, opened in Safari in the person's own session.
  • Everything App Review asks of a container app stays: what it is, how to enable it, support and privacy, licences.

One call for the maintainer

With Pipeup on, the button opens the sample page. The alternative is no button at all once Pipeup is on, just the line and the footer. The sample page gives a first-timer somewhere to press the button straight away, which is why it is the proposal.
