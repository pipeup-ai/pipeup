Assist: suggested replies from a model on your device

A reviewer can ask for a suggestion on a comment. A small AI model that runs on their own machine reads the comment and the part of the page it is about, finds related passages elsewhere in the document, and drafts a reply. Nothing leaves the device, and nothing is posted unless the reviewer posts it. Working name: Assist.

1. The first time: asked first, in plain words

Nothing runs until the reviewer says yes. The panel says where the model is, what it costs the machine, and that nothing leaves it. Two cases: the browser already has a model, or one has to be downloaded.

An error. No codes or stack traces; a way forward.

What changes for people who don't use it: nothing. The add-on is a separate script. A page without it, or a reviewer who never turns it on, sees no new button, loads no model and uses no extra memory.
