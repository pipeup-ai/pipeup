Visual spec · agreed and built 9 November 2026

Write where you point; pin the comments when you want them beside the page

Picking something to comment on should put the cursor in the comment box straight away. The browser won't move the cursor from the page into its side panel, so the box opens on the page, as a popover beside what you picked. The side panel becomes something you choose: pinned, it stays beside the page with every comment; unpinned, comments open as popovers on the page and nothing pushes the page aside.

3. The spec change

  • Choosing something to comment on opens the comment box on the page, beside it, with the cursor in the box.
  • Comments can be pinned beside the page, in the browser's side panel, or not. Unpinned (the default), a comment's thread opens beside it on the page, and the side panel opens only from Comments in the toolbar panel. Pinned, the side panel stays open, threads open there, and new comments show there once sent.
  • The pin is in the side panel's header and in the toolbar panel (Keep comments beside the page); the choice is kept for every site. Closing the side panel unpins.

4. Decisions (agreed 9 November 2026)

Unpinned by default? Agreed: yes. The page stays as its author made it, and the first comment is a click and some typing. The other choice is pinned by default, which shows every comment from the start but narrows the page.

Writing always in a popover, even pinned? Agreed: yes, because that's the only way the cursor can land in the box. The other choice is writing in the side panel when pinned, which needs a click into the box first.
